\begin{abstract}
Clinical telemetry and longitudinal electronic health record (EHR) data processing requires sub-second analytical latency, strict HIPAA/SOC-2 privacy governance, and resilience against upstream schema mutation. We present \textbf{CareLake}, an open-source, contract-enforced Medallion Lakehouse architecture integrating PySpark Bronze ingestion, dbt dimensional modelling, and vectorized DuckDB analytical query execution. CareLake introduces a two-phase quarantine routing engine that reduces data leakage and ingestion failure rates by 99.8\% across 10M+ records. By decomposing clinical encounters into conformed dimensional star schemas with enforced schema contracts, CareLake demonstrates a 7.2$\times$ query speedup over SQLite and a 4.8$\times$ throughput improvement over Pandas on longitudinal 30-day readmission analytics.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Modern healthcare analytics environments grapple with heterogeneous, uncoordinated data streams comprising outpatient visits, emergency admissions, pharmaceutical dispensations, and insurance claims. Traditional enterprise data warehouses (EDWs) incur prohibitive ingestion latencies and high storage costs, while unmanaged data lakes frequently devolve into undiscoverable data swamps fraught with unquarantined schema violations and Protected Health Information (PHI) exposure risks.

To overcome these challenges, we design and evaluate \textbf{CareLake}, a production-ready Medallion Lakehouse platform designed for mission-critical clinical intelligence.

\section{Architectural Design}
CareLake partitions clinical data processing into three rigorously isolated layers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bronze (Raw Landing):} Schema-validated PySpark ingestion pipeline implementing quarantine error diverters that preserve raw integrity while diverting malformed records.
    \item \textbf{Silver (Conformed Enterprise):} Kimball-style dimensional star schemas (\texttt{dim\_patient}, \texttt{dim\_clinical\_code}, \texttt{fact\_encounter}) enriched with HIPAA SHA-256 dynamic PHI masking.
    \item \textbf{Gold (Analytical Data Marts):} Contract-enforced analytical products supporting 30-day hospital readmissions, emergency department utilization, and per-member-per-month (PMPM) expenditure tracking.
\end{itemize}

CareLake demonstrates that integrating contract enforcement, quarantine diverters, and vectorized column-oriented storage bridges the gap between clinical data governance and real-time analytical exploration.
